# Shijima agent product review and recovery plan

> **Implementation handoff:** [2026-09-23-product-fidelity-handoff.md](2026-09-23-product-fidelity-handoff.md) is the current full product contract. This shorter review contains proposals (including fee and starter-credit interpretations), not approved policy. Use the handoff's authority, parity and acceptance sections for build work.

**23 September 2026.** Read-only audit of `main` at `b529ef7`, the running local `/desk/showcase`, this repository's decision and build records, Masayume's copy implementation, and Stocklana/Agari source and plans. This is the next product contract, not a claim that these features are shipped. No mainnet transaction, deployment, OpenServ publication, or faucet transfer was made for this review.

## Recommendation

Make **Agents** the product's primary action: discover a live agent, inspect its decision record and portfolio, then create your own or copy it into a separate account you own. Keep **Markets** first in discovery navigation. Restore a landing page at `/` for everyone, including signed-in users. Treat each agent's contract as its wallet: USDG enters that contract, its bounded operator trades from it, and only its owner can withdraw. Show that route, all fees, ETH gas responsibility, and the receipt before asking anyone to go live.

The current `Desk` contract and worker can support the owner-account safety model, but **copy execution, creator fee collection, a one-time mainnet credit, and a 20-strategy catalog are not implemented**. Do not describe those as live. The current site is on Robinhood Chain mainnet (4663), while the showcase is in practice mode; those are separate facts.

### Why the current page feels wrong

The agent card contains four small counters and a latest-decision excerpt; the next column starts with another balance/holdings summary, then another value summary/chart, then allocation and holdings as separate blocks. The most interesting event is buried in Activity. On desktop the lower tabs visually belong to the value column. On mobile the two tab systems add more navigation to reach a decision. `AgentCard`, `Plate`, `ValueChart`, `Allocation`, `Holdings`, `DeskSections` and `DeskTabs` are the specific composition owners. This is an information hierarchy problem as well as a responsive layout problem; a new carousel alone will not repair it.

## Proposed journeys

1. **Discover:** `/` explains the product and stays available after sign-in. Markets is the first discovery nav destination; Agents is the primary action destination. A small, manually controlled carousel shows verified live agents, not fabricated performance. Each card says owner/creator, strategy, live/practice status, last decision, time, costs and risk, with Open agent and Copy agent actions. If there are no eligible live agents, explain why and offer practice/create.
2. **Inspect:** `/agents/[slug]` leads with name, creator, live/practice state, **latest decision and reason**, transaction link when one exists, and Copy agent. A single portfolio module includes value chart, allocation and expandable holdings; a decision timeline follows. Activity is the full ledger, not a duplicate summary. Owner controls and Settings are prominent for the owner's agent. Keep the working share card and verified decision proof.
3. **Copy:** choose a published leader, see its rules and history, enter a follower budget and tighter limits, see the current creator fee and estimated trading/bridge/network costs, fund a **new owner-controlled follower account**, then activate. The leader's signal is a candidate; follower code must re-read prices, scale size to its own budget, pass that follower's token allowlist, caps, slippage/oracle/loss gates, and record an independent result. A paused, stale, failed or unfunded leader/follower cannot silently place a copy. Followers can pause, stop copying and withdraw without creator permission.
4. **Create:** choose a verified strategy, connect/sign in, bring USDG from Robinhood Chain or a supported origin chain, review contract address and expected USDG/ETH balances, create, fund, then choose practice or live. “Practice” means decisions without trades, even though the account may exist on mainnet. Show the exact point live trading starts. Recover an interrupted create or deposit from chain receipts before suggesting another send.
5. **Connect:** Settings → Connections shows owner wallet, agent account, Telegram and OpenServ. OpenServ uses the existing one-time workspace link and real read/ask capability. A workflow may request “check now” through the same bounded engine; money-moving requests produce a wallet confirmation in Shijima. OpenServ's Base ERC-8004/workspace identity is distinct from the Robinhood Chain trading account.

## Mainnet, funding, fee and credit design

**Money ownership.** `DeskFactory.createDesk` sets `owner = msg.sender`; `Desk.withdraw` always transfers to that owner. Funding is a USDG ERC-20 transfer to the agent contract, including when a bridge delivers it. The contract has no `deposit()` call, so the product should say **Add USDG to your agent** and show the contract address and confirmed balance. Do not call the browser wallet itself the agent wallet. The creator never receives custody of follower principal.

**Gas.** Robinhood Chain 4663 uses ETH for gas, not USDG. Automated actions use the service's operator ETH. Creation, owner management and direct funding require owner ETH unless a tested sponsor path pays it. The optional cross-chain ETH top-up in `BridgeIn` is for the owner's wallet, not the agent contract. Operator gas has a low-balance refusal and monitoring path; expose its operational state without implying that a balance monitor guarantees execution. The product needs a fee preview and a clear “who pays” line for each transaction. [Robinhood Chain network docs](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/connecting/).

**“Free trading.”** Recommend **zero Shijima platform fee for beta followers and creators** until the creator-fee accounting and payout contract are audited and accepted. This cannot mean zero Uniswap pool fee, price impact, bridge charge, ETH gas or potential loss. For creator fees, Masayume's fixed subscription fee is a working reference. A fixed, disclosed fee charged on join/resume is simpler to attribute than performance fees; whether it is charged during beta needs the owner's decision. Do not reuse `Desk.tokenCfg.fee`: it is the pinned Uniswap pool tier. The existing 0.5% annual display is currently waived and has no collection route.

**One-time mainnet starter credit.** The spoken amounts conflict: $1 USDG once, then “start with 20”. Treat the exact free amount as pending confirmation. My proposed interpretation is **$1 USDG once per eligible person** and **$20 as the suggested user deposit** for useful live trading. A $1 credit cannot by itself satisfy a $20 start rule, and current code's $5 minimum also conflicts. Set a single product rule before advertising the credit. If credit is meant to let someone execute a real trade, model the actual minimum order, pool impact and operator cost first; otherwise describe it as an onboarding credit or use a separate paper practice balance. A mainnet faucet needs a treasury budget, durable per-user eligibility/claim ledger, identity/Sybil controls, rate and global caps, transfer reconciliation, and an exhausted/failed state. USDG credit does not pay ETH gas. No faucet or one-time eligibility mechanism exists today.

**Market eligibility.** Stock Tokens are restricted in several jurisdictions, and the issuer describes them as debt securities rather than equity ownership. Put the issuer's current eligibility and product terms into the live onboarding review before offering a mainnet credit or copy. [Robinhood Stock Tokens](https://docs.robinhood.com/chain/stock-tokens/). The 20 strategies must each resolve to currently tradable allowlisted token addresses, valid feeds and sufficient pool depth; 20 names built from five identical baskets would not satisfy this request.

## OpenServ integration decision

The existing implementation is more than a logo: agent 4513 is provisioned, an OpenServ workspace can be linked by code, and linked chat/tasks use Shijima's ask path. The local prior audit records one linked workspace answer, but that does not prove a public marketplace listing or every user's connection. The 2025 platform transcript describes workflows, agents, integrations, MCPs and sessions; it is dated. Current [OpenServ full-stack guidance](https://docs.openserv.ai/vibecode/openclaw/fullstack-app) documents backend webhook triggers and runnable external agents. Use the existing SDK integration and a webhook only where it adds an observable run; do not replace deterministic trade authorization with an unbounded MCP call. A public MCP can be considered for **read-only agent discovery, portfolio and verified decision queries** after per-user authorization is designed. OpenServ OAuth login is unverified; do not make it the product's account binding.

The platform and app should show a consistent connection state: not linked, code ready, linked to this workspace, expired, and revoked. Add a visible OpenServ mark using their approved asset/usage guidance, label “Decisions use SERV Reasoning”, and link the agent identity. A linked OpenServ workspace must not become a withdrawal signer or obtain an operator key. Public availability requires a separate marketplace approval/status check.

## 21st design direction

Keep the Masayume/Agari typography, color and tokens. The `21st review apps/web --json` run found 0 errors, 11 warnings and many low-severity suggestions; it cannot judge the page hierarchy above. Catalog searches found an accessible Embla-based [Interfaces Carousel](https://21st.dev/@jshguo/components/interfaces-carousel) and [Empty State](https://21st.dev/@serafimcloud/components/empty-state). Inspect their code, dependencies, keyboard behavior and licenses before adapting. Use a manual carousel with visible next/previous and no forced autoplay for live agents. The carousel is discovery; the decision and copy flow still need first-class screens. Review desktop and 390/768px states with real short/long names, 0/1/many agents, no history, pending transaction, low gas, and unavailable data.

## Build sequence and acceptance

1. **Navigation and page hierarchy:** make `/` stable for signed-in users; Markets → Agents → Strategies; branded 404; visible agent Settings. Rename user-facing desk text. Move latest decision and Activity into the agent region, unify portfolio/holdings, and repair empty and mobile states. Accept when a signed-in user can reach Home in one tap and the latest decision/Copy action is visible without searching through the value tabs.
2. **Money journey:** draw owner wallet, agent contract and operator wallet in the UI/docs; consolidate Create/Add/Bridge/Withdraw into one clear state machine with receipts and recovery. State and enforce one minimum. Accept on a mainnet fork: create, fund, interrupted funding, no ETH, bridge pending, partial arrival, add, pause, withdraw; no duplicate transfer after an uncertain result.
3. **Agent catalog:** define strategy schema (asset universe, weights or signal rule, cadence, limits, creator, fee, status, version); publish only verified entries. Seed and validate 20 meaningfully different strategies, each with a human-readable purpose and live/practice availability. Show a truthful empty catalog before the target is reached.
4. **Copy engine and fees:** publish leader actions; create independent follower accounts, bounded subscription and revocation, deterministic follower sizing/gates, durable per-follower job/receipt, missed-signal handling and replay protection. Define and implement creator fee terms separately from pool fees, including payout/refund and fee changes for existing followers. Accept on a fork with two followers of different balances/limits: one trade is copied at independent sizes, one is refused safely, both have their own tx/decision records, and pause/withdraw works when the leader is unavailable.
5. **OpenServ and credits:** make current link discoverable, verify link/revoke across two workspaces, expose read/ask/check-now safely, verify marketplace status, then implement and budget the one-time mainnet credit after the amount and eligibility rule is settled. Mainnet transfer and public promotion are separate release decisions.
6. **Release proof:** run a full signed-in and visitor walkthrough at phone/tablet/desktop sizes, run the contract/worker test gates, rehearse new money paths on a fork, then run an attended small-value mainnet acceptance with explicit owner authorization. Record actual gas payer, token transfer, trade receipt, follower limits and withdrawal. Do not infer live-copy readiness from paper mode, source presence or passing isolated tests.

## Decisions that need the owner

1. Does the one-time credit mean **$1 USDG free** and **$20 suggested user deposit**, or a different funded amount/rule?
2. For creator revenue, is a **fixed disclosed join/resume fee** acceptable, or do you want another fee model? Should it be collected during beta or shown/waived until the flow is proven?
3. Should a copy follow the leader's **individual eligible trades** (the recommendation above), the leader's current basket/weights, or both as distinct actions?

These questions do not block navigation, agent-page, empty-state, 404, or funding-journey repair. They do block a truthful claim that the starter credit and paid copy flow are live.
