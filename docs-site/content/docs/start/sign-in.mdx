Shijima never holds your keys or your money. You sign in with a wallet you already have.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open the wallet picker

Press **Connect wallet**. The picker, **Connect a wallet**, lists every wallet it finds in this browser under **In this browser**, such as MetaMask or Rabby. If it finds none, it says so and links to MetaMask and Rabby.

<Clip name="start/sign-in" caption="Connect wallet opens the picker; this browser has no wallet installed." />

<Shot name="start/sign-in-picker" alt="The Connect a wallet picker says no wallet was found in this browser and offers MetaMask, Rabby and WalletConnect." caption="The picker explains what to do when this browser has no wallet installed." />
</Step>
<Step>
### Or use your phone

Choose <Named name="walletconnect" /> to use a wallet on your phone: MetaMask, Rainbow, Trust, Coinbase and hundreds more. Scan the QR code under **Scan with your phone**, or press **Copy link**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Prove it is your wallet

Press **Sign to continue** and sign one message in your wallet. It is a signature, not a transaction: **it costs nothing and moves nothing.** The message names this site and Robinhood Chain (4663), so it cannot be reused anywhere else.
</Step>
</Steps>

You stay signed in for 7 days on this browser.

## The right network

Shijima runs only on <Named name="robinhood" />. When you sign in, it asks your wallet to switch there first. If your wallet refuses, sign-in still works, and a banner says **Wrong network** with a **Switch to Robinhood Chain** button. You need to be on Robinhood Chain before you sign a transaction.

<Callout title="WalletConnect on a self-hosted copy">
The WalletConnect row only appears when the site has a WalletConnect project id (`NEXT_PUBLIC_WALLETCONNECT_PROJECT_ID`). Without one, browser wallets still work.
</Callout>
