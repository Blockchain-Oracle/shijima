**Live** means your agent trades for real, inside your limits. **Practice** means it looks, thinks and decides exactly as it would live, then writes down what it *would have* done and spends nothing.

New agents start **Live** unless you choose Practice when you create one. You can switch either way at any time, in your agent's **Settings**, under **Trading**. No transaction is needed to switch.

<Shot name="start/live-or-practice-practice" alt="The Amount step has Practice with no money selected and shows the agent starting with no deposit." caption="Practice starts with no money and can be funded later." />

## Which to choose

| | Live | Practice |
|---|---|---|
| Looks at your account and the market | yes | yes |
| Asks SERV Reasoning when to act | yes | yes |
| Writes every decision in the record | yes | yes, as “would have” |
| Spends money | yes, inside your limits | never |
| Telegram tells you | “I did this” | “I would have done this” |
| Idle cash goes to the savings vault | when it acts on its own | no |

Practice is useful when you want to watch how it thinks before trusting it with money, or when you have not funded it yet.

## Why live is the default

Your money's protections are the same in every mode. Your contract refuses any trade over your per-trade or per-day limit, and only your wallet can ever withdraw. A waiting period in Practice added friction, not safety, so it was removed on 25 September. Your agent's practice checks are still counted and shown as its track record.

Live has two flavours, **Ask me first** and **On its own**. See [modes](/agent/modes).
