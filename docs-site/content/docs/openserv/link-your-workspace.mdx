You do not need OpenServ to use Shijima. This is for people who already work in an <Named name="openserv" /> workspace and want their agent there too. There is no “Sign in with OpenServ”; instead you link a workspace with a one-time code.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Add Shijima to OpenServ
In your agent's **Settings**, open **Connections**, then the **OpenServ agent** card. Step 1, **Add Shijima to OpenServ**: press **Open Shijima on OpenServ**. It opens agent 4513 on the platform. Add it to a workspace of yours.
</Step>
<Step>
### Make a link code
Step 2, **Link your workspace**: press **Make a link code**. The page shows the line to send, for example `link ABC12345`, with **Copy**. The code **works once, for 30 minutes**, and the page counts down.
</Step>
<Step>
### Send it in your workspace
Paste the line into Shijima's chat in your workspace. Shijima answers “Linked. This workspace now talks to …”. On the website, the card shows your workspace under **Linked workspaces** within seconds.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What you can do from there

Ask in the workspace chat, or as a task:

```text
How is my portfolio doing?
Why did you wait on Nvidia?
Check now
```

The answers come from the same brain as the website and Telegram. **Anything that moves money comes back as a link** to confirm on your agent's page. Nothing changes from OpenServ alone.

## Options, per workspace

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Let it ask for a look now** | Lets agents in your workspace wake yours with `check_now`. Your limits still decide; it can never withdraw or raise a limit. At most once every ten minutes. |
| **Send decisions to your workspace** | Paste a webhook trigger URL from OpenServ (`https://api.openserv.ai/webhooks/trigger/…`) and press **Save**. After each check, your agent posts its decisions to it, which starts that workflow. |
| **Unlink** | Ends the link. |

Only OpenServ webhook URLs are accepted, so a saved webhook can never make Shijima post anywhere else. They are stored encrypted.

Every decision that OpenServ asked for says so on its record, with the workspace, the task and the run that asked.
