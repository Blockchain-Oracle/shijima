## How copying works

Copying gives you **your own agent**, in your own contract, with your own money. When the agent you copy makes a trade, yours makes the same move **as a share of its own value**.

<Example>
The agent you copy holds **$500** and puts **12%** of it, $60, into Nvidia. Your agent holds **$50**, so it puts 12% of its money, **$6**, into Nvidia.
</Example>

- The agent you copy can never touch your money.
- **Your** limits, cash, largest-holding setting and mode apply to every copy. In Practice your agent copies on paper; in Ask me first it asks you.
- No AI call is made for a copy. The other agent already made the timing call.
- A move yours cannot make is written down as a **missed copy**, with the reason: not enough cash, no holding to sell, over your daily limit, over your largest-holding setting, too small to be worth the fee, or older than 30 minutes by the time it was seen.
- Pause, resume or stop from your agent's page. Moves made while you were paused are **never** copied later.

## Start copying

<Steps>
<Step>
### Copy this agent
On a public agent whose owner has enabled copying, press **Copy this agent**, then **Continue: set up your agent**. If the owner has not enabled it, the button explains that copying is unavailable.

<Shot name="agent/copy-unavailable" alt="The Copy this agent dialog says its owner has not opened this agent to copying." caption="An agent's owner can leave copying closed." />
</Step>
<Step>
### Create your agent
The studio opens with the other agent's mix, under the banner **Copying** and its name. Set your amount and limits, and create it as usual.
</Step>
<Step>
### Pay the fee, if there is one
**Last step: start copying.** If the creator set a fee, you pay it in USDG from your wallet: **Pay $X to the creator** and **Pay $Y to Shijima**. The server checks both transfers on the chain before it links the copy. Then **Start copying**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## The fee

A creator can ask a **one-time fee of $0 to $5**, shown before you sign. It is split **80% to the creator, 20% to Shijima**. Shijima takes nothing else: no fee per trade and none on what you hold.

<Example>
A creator sets **$5**. You pay **$4 to the creator** and **$1 to Shijima**, once. After that, copying costs nothing but your own agent's network fees, which Shijima pays.
</Example>

## Let others copy yours

In your agent's **Settings**, under **Sharing**, turn on its public link, then **Let others copy this agent**, and set your fee.
