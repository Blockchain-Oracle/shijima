Press **⌘J** (Ctrl+J on Windows), or the chat button at the bottom right, on any page. **Esc** closes it. It talks about the agent on screen, or your first agent.

## What you can ask

- “How is my portfolio doing?”
- “Why did you wait on Nvidia?”
- “Check now.”
- “Sell half my Tesla if it falls 4%.”
- “Switch me to Ask me first.”

It answers from your agent's own record and settings, and cites them. It cannot see anything else, and it will not quote facts it was not given.

## It proposes, you confirm

When you ask for a change, it shows a **card** with exactly what would change. Press **Confirm** or **Not now**. A card expires after 10 minutes. Who has to confirm depends on what it touches:

| What it proposes | You confirm with |
|---|---|
| Change strategy, weights, notes, agent limits or rules · pause, resume, change mode · answer a request · check now · price alerts · “do it anyway” | your signed-in session |
| Withdraw · remove the assistant · lower the limits on the chain | your browser's session key, if you set one up |
| Sell everything · sell some · unpause the contract · add money · close the agent | your wallet |

Raising the limits on the chain always needs your wallet.

## “Do it anyway”

If your agent chose to wait, you can overrule it from the chat. It is a second confirmation, and the trade still goes through every limit check and your contract. The record marks it as **your** call, not your agent's, and it is left out of your agent's timing grades. It is not available in Practice.

## Limits on chatting

6 messages a minute and 150 a day, each up to 1,000 characters.

## Where the answers come from

Your message is saved, and the worker answers it with one call to SERV Reasoning. The same brain answers in [Telegram](/agent/telegram) and in [your OpenServ workspace](/openserv/link-your-workspace).
