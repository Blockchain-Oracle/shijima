| What | Who pays | Roughly |
|---|---|---|
| A trade, vault move or daily seal by your agent | **Shijima**, from the operator's ETH | about 5¢ each |
| Creating your agent | You, in ETH from your wallet | about 30¢ |
| Adding money, withdrawing, sending, bridging | You, in ETH from your wallet | a few cents each |
| Moving money across chains | You, to Relay | about 1% all in |
| Copying an agent with a fee | You, once, in USDG | $0 to $5, 80% to the creator, 20% to Shijima |
| Trading, or holding money in Shijima | nobody | free |

Every screen shows the network fee before you sign.

<Example>
You put in **$20** and your agent makes **10 trades** this month. You paid about **30¢** to create it and a few cents to fund it. Shijima paid about **50¢** for the 10 trades. Shijima took nothing from your $20.
</Example>

## Out of ETH?

Open [Bridge → Get gas](/money/bridge#get-gas): swap $1 of USDG to ETH, or bring ETH from another chain. Or claim [the free $1](/start/free-dollar), which includes some.

## Why no fee per trade

An agent that is paid per trade is paid to trade too much. Shijima's only income is its 20% share of copy fees.
