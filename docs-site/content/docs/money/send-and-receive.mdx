## Send

<AppLink href="/send">Send</AppLink> moves **USDG, ETH or a Stock Token** from your own wallet to any address on Robinhood Chain.

- Paste the address, or press **SCAN** to read a QR code with your camera. Nothing leaves your device.
- It refuses a **token contract** as the recipient: money sent to a token contract is lost.
- It points you to **Fund** if the address is one of your own agents, so the money counts as money in.

<Shot name="money/send" alt="The Send screen has a token amount, recipient address field, Scan button and summary before Review." caption="Check the token, amount and recipient before review." />

<Callout type="warn" title="Sends cannot be undone">
Check the address. Anything sent to the wrong one is gone, and everyone can see the transfer.
</Callout>

## Receive

**Receive** opens a sheet with your wallet's address, or one of your agents', as text and a QR code, to be paid on Robinhood Chain.

Only USDG and Stock Tokens should go to an agent's address. **Never send ETH to an agent**: its contract cannot receive it.
