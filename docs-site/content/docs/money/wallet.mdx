When you sign in, <AppLink href="/wallet">Wallet</AppLink> is home. It shows two things, read from the chain just now:

<Shot name="money/wallet-overview" lightOnly alt="A connected wallet shows one agent's holdings beside the wallet's USDG and ETH balances." caption="A connected wallet overview, captured in light mode." />

- **Your agents**: all the money your agents hold, as cash, stocks and savings. Tap to see each agent.
- **Your wallet**, on Robinhood Chain: every token in it, priced, with a total.

The buttons move money between them: **Fund an agent**, **Receive**, **Withdraw**, **Send**, **Bridge**, and **Evidence**.

## Two accounts, one owner

| | Your wallet | Your agent's account |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | The wallet you signed in with | A contract created for you by the agent factory |
| Who controls it | You | You own it; Shijima's operator key may trade inside your limits |
| Who can take money out | You | Only you, and only to your wallet |
| Holds | Anything | USDG, the Stock Tokens it trades, and savings-vault shares |

<Diagram name="money" />

## What your agent holds

- **Cash**: USDG, a digital dollar on Robinhood Chain.
- **Stocks**: Stock Tokens from its approved list of 16.
- **Savings**: idle cash in the Steakhouse USDG vault on <Named name="morpho" />, when your agent acts on its own. It is taken back out when a buy needs it. The rate changes; your agent shows the live one.

<Callout type="warn" title="Never send ETH to an agent">
Your agent's contract cannot receive ETH. Send it USDG, or use Fund, which swaps ETH to USDG on the way in.
</Callout>
