Open <AppLink href="/withdraw">Withdraw</AppLink>, choose **From which agent**, then **What to take out**:

| Choice | Options | Where it ends up |
|---|---|---|
| **Cash** | Some or all. Leave it empty for all of it. | Your wallet |
| **One stock** | **Take it as it is** | Your wallet, as the Stock Token |
| | **Sell it to cash** | Your agent, as cash. Take the cash out after. |
| **Everything** | **As cash, selling first** | Your wallet, as USDG |
| | **As it is, stocks and all** | Your wallet, each token as it is |

Cash in savings comes out of the vault first when needed. Sales are never worse than 1% under the quote.

## To your wallet only

Your agent's contract has one way to pay out, `withdraw`, and it always pays **its owner**. There is no address field to get wrong and no way for anyone to redirect it: not Shijima, not the agent's key.

After it lands, **Send it on** or **Bridge it out** from your wallet.

## Withdrawing is not a loss

Money in and out is recorded apart from trading. A $2 withdrawal shows as $2 out, not as a $2 loss, and your loss stop counts from the new amount.
