<AppLink href="/bridge">Bridge</AppLink> moves money between Robinhood Chain and <Named name="base" />, <Named name="arbitrum" />, <Named name="ethereum" /> or <Named name="bnb" />, through <Named name="relay" />. It has three tabs.

## In

Brings money **into your own wallet** on Robinhood Chain from another network. Choose what it **Arrives as**:

- **USDG**, to spend or fund an agent.
- **ETH**, for network fees.

Nobody else can be the recipient. To put money straight into an agent instead, use [Fund](/money/fund) → Another chain.

## Out

Takes **USDG** from your wallet on Robinhood Chain to **the same wallet** on another chain, arriving as a token Relay offers there, such as USDC. Nobody else can be the recipient. If your USDG is still in an agent, [withdraw](/money/withdraw) it first.

## Get gas

Every move you sign on Robinhood Chain costs a few cents of ETH. **Get gas** gives you two ways:

- **Swap $1 of USDG to ETH**, on Uniswap, into your own wallet.
- **Bring ETH from another chain**, which arrives as ETH on Robinhood Chain.

Or claim [the free $1](/start/free-dollar) while it lasts, which includes some gas.

## How a bridge moves

1. The server asks Relay for a price and shows you every step.
2. Your wallet signs. Nothing moves before that.
3. Relay fills it on the other side, and the app follows it to the end. **On its way** means Relay is still delivering; it usually lands within a minute.

Relay costs about 1% all in. The least you can bridge is $1, but $20 or more is better value, because part of the fee is fixed. Relay does not carry Stock Tokens.
