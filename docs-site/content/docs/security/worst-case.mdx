## If the operator key were stolen

The thief could **not** take your money out. They could only make bad trades inside your contract's guards.

Every guarded trade must return at least 92% of the Chainlink value. Each dollar traded counts against your daily limit. So the most they could lose you is about **8% of your daily limit in each daily window, plus pool fees**. The window is fixed, so across the boundary between two windows that can happen twice in a row: **up to about 16% of your daily limit** in a short time.

<Example>
Your per-day limit is **$50**. A thief with the operator key makes the worst trades the contract allows. They lose you at most about **$4** in one window, plus pool fees, or about **$8** if they time it across two windows. Then you press **Remove the assistant**, and it stops.
</Example>

The same bound applies to a session key, for the few days it lives. Vault moves are not counted against your limits, but the money stays in your contract.

## Other honest limits

- **The contract is not audited.** It is small, has no upgrade path and no admin. Amounts are small.
- **One operator key serves every agent today.** The contract already supports a key per agent.
- **Weekend moves over 8%.** If a price moves more than 8% from the last feed update, your agent's trades are refused by your contract by design. Only you can sell then. The record says so.
- **Your agent predicts nothing.** It claims no edge. Weekend prices are a poor guide to Monday in either direction.
- **Stock Tokens are not shares.** No ownership of the company and no shareholder rights.
- **Savings can be slow to leave.** Taking cash out of the vault depends on what the vault has available at that moment.
- **If Shijima's worker stops**, your agent stops looking. Your money stays where it is, and you can still withdraw, even [without our website](/security/withdraw-without-us).
